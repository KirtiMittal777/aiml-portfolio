Professional Bio

Frontend developer learning to build AI that customers can trust

I'm Kirti Mittal. For about ten years I have built web applications for clients and companies, mostly with React, TypeScript, Next.js, and Angular. Much of that work has been e-commerce: product pages, carts, and checkout flows where a confusing screen costs real sales.

In 2026 I started a Master of Science in Computer Science with an AI specialization at Indiana Wesleyan University. More of the products I work on now include AI features, and I want to understand how they work, where they fail, and how to design them so people can rely on them.
